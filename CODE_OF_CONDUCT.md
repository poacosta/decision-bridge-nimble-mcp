# Code of Conduct

This project is for people building and using local decision tooling. To keep it welcoming, everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, and discussions agrees to the following.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Reviews are about the code and the docs.
- Help others get unstuck, especially newcomers.
- Keep reports and examples free of other people's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention directed at a person.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests, lock conversations, and block people who break these rules.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately. The private contact is published in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Until it is, open an issue that says only that you want to report a conduct concern, with no details, and the maintainer will arrange a private channel.
